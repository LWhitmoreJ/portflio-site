UI/UX & Prototyping

Design Work

My UI/UX work spans game interface design, web application wireframes, interactive prototypes, and design system documentation.

My Design Process

01. Research

Understanding the user, the goal, and the constraints before a single wireframe is drawn.

02. Wireframe

Low-fidelity layouts that establish structure, hierarchy, and content flow.

03. Prototype

High-fidelity interactive prototypes in Figma that simulate the real user experience.

04. Iterate

Refining based on feedback until the design solves the original problem clearly.

This portfolio website went through a full UX design process before a being created. Audience research, persona development, wireframing in Figma, a site map diagram, a complete design system, and high-fidelity mockups.

The challenge was designing for both game industry recruiters who scan quickly and freelance clients who read carefully. The solution was a layered navigation architecture and a visual hierarchy that leads with work samples.
